# Initial Validation Plan

## Objective

The initial validation goal is to test whether MedEd-HalluScore can be applied consistently by reviewers and whether its risk levels align with expert judgment about educational safety.

## Phase 1: Feasibility Review

Sample:

- 30 LLM-generated clinical cases
- 5 to 10 common medical education topics
- At least 2 reviewers when possible

Outputs:

- Completion time per case
- Reviewer feedback on rubric clarity
- Distribution of scores by dimension
- Examples of ambiguous scoring decisions

## Phase 2: Inter-Rater Reliability

Sample:

- 50 to 100 cases
- At least 2 independent reviewers
- Optional third reviewer for adjudication

Suggested analyses:

- Percent agreement by dimension
- Weighted Cohen's kappa for two reviewers
- Intraclass correlation coefficient for total score
- Agreement on final risk category

## Phase 3: Expert Alignment

Compare MedEd-HalluScore risk levels with a global expert rating:

- Safe after minor edits
- Requires major revision
- Reject or rewrite

Primary question:

Does a higher MedEd-HalluScore correspond to a higher expert-rated educational safety concern?

## Phase 4: External Pilot

Invite medical educators or clinical faculty outside the original author group to apply the rubric to a small set of cases.

Desired evidence:

- Independent reviewer feedback
- Documented use in teaching material review
- Pull requests, issues, or rubric refinements
- Letters or statements from external users

## Reporting

The first validation report should include:

- Dataset description
- Reviewer qualifications
- Scoring instructions
- Statistical methods
- Results
- Known limitations
- Planned revisions
